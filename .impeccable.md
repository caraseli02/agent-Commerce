## Design Context

### Users

Components Marketplace is aimed at a general demo audience reviewing a frontend engineering portfolio. Reviewers should be able to understand and explore the storefront, account, and dashboard flows without specialist electronics knowledge or external services.

### Brand Personality

Simple, modern, and helpful. The interface should feel clear, trustworthy, and easy to evaluate rather than experimental or visually theatrical.

### Aesthetic Direction

A polished, light, contemporary commerce interface that foregrounds product discovery and demonstrates frontend breadth. It should avoid flashy portfolio-only effects, generic AI decoration, and unnecessary complexity.

### Design Principles

1. Make the primary review flows immediately understandable to a first-time visitor.
2. Prefer clear hierarchy, restrained styling, and purposeful interaction over decoration.
3. Demonstrate engineering depth through reliable responsive, accessible, and stateful flows.
4. Keep the demo self-contained and make its demo-only behavior explicit.
5. Maintain visual and interaction consistency across storefront and dashboard surfaces.

### Visual System ("Bench")

Functional instrument design: instrument-gray surfaces, graphite ink, and one signal-orange key. Tokens live in `app/assets/css/main.css`.

- **Ink (`primary`)**: default buttons, checked controls, selected borders, active tabs. Inverts in dark mode, so pair it with `text-primary-foreground`, never `text-white`.
- **Signal (`signal`, `signal-foreground`, `signal-text`)**: reserved for the decisive commerce action on a screen (search submit, product page add to cart, checkout) via `UiButton variant="cart"`, plus small indicators (cart count, active-tab bar, unread dot). `signal-text` is the 4.5:1 orange for links. Do not use orange as decoration or for prices.
- **Brand**: a clean Components Marketplace wordmark; use the CM monogram only in compact navigation and the favicon.
- **Status colours** stay semantic: green stock and success, amber low stock, red errors and backorder, blue only for informational and categorical states.
- **Type**: Hanken Grotesk for all UI text (tabular figures by default); JetBrains Mono only for codes such as part numbers and order IDs.
- **Surfaces**: resting cards use hairline `border-border`; shadows are for raised layers (menus, sheets, popovers). The dashboard sidebar uses the `sidebar` neutral layer.
