# Demo product imagery

`rs485-package.webp` and `schottky-package.webp` are AI-generated, unbranded studio-style package images created for this portfolio demo on 2026-10-20. They represent SOIC-20 and SOT-23 packages respectively, not authenticated photographs of ADM2587E or BAT54C parts. The gallery discloses their representative nature. Verify package, dimensions and markings against the product specifications before any real sourcing decision.

All three images are 1000 × 1000 WebP assets, optimized from generated PNG originals. No third-party manufacturer logo or product marking was added. `microcontroller.webp` was edited with imagegen from the existing representative demo image to remove its beige circuit-board backdrop and match the white studio framing.

The ADM2587E mock package and isolation labels follow the [Analog Devices product specification](https://www.analog.com/en/products/adm2587e.html): 20-lead wide-body SOIC and 2.5 kV isolation.
